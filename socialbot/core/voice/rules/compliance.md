# Compliance rules (hard gates, not voice)

HIC is a nonpartisan 501(c)(3). These rules are enforced in code where possible and must be
followed by every draft regardless of voice.

1. **No candidate, race, or election framing.** Do not name a candidate for office, compare
   candidates, reference voting or an election, or praise or criticize anyone's campaign. The US
   midterms are November 3, 2026. Stories flagged `election` are never drafted automatically.
2. **Until November 4, 2026: elected officials by role only, never by name.** Many state attorneys
   general, governors and members of Congress are on the ballot. Write "Iowa's attorney general",
   "a Kansas official", "a US senator", not their names. Quote their words if useful, attributed to
   the role. After the election this relaxes to rule 3.
3. **Officials by role and action.** When an officeholder acts (a bill, a hearing, a lawsuit, an
   enforcement action), report the action and their own words. No party labels in our voice, no
   praise, no scorekeeping of who signed what, no "bipartisan" as our word.
4. **Never name a deceased minor or a bereaved family.** Write "a 14-year-old", "the teen's parents".
5. **Attribute contested claims.** Lawsuits allege. Reports find. Companies say. The states say.
6. **Anchor policy commentary to the action**, not to an election: a filing, a rulemaking, a hearing.
7. **Calls to action that ask people to contact legislators about a specific bill** are lobbying.
   They are allowed but must be labeled `c3-advocacy` so the spend is logged. Default CTA is
   AI Reality Check, which is not lobbying.
8. **Images:** real photos, screenshots, graphic design and illustration are fine. Nothing generated
   may look like a real photograph, a real person, or a real place.

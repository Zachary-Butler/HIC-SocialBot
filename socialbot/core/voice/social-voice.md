# HIC social voice (v0 seed, Oct 16 2026)

Owner: Zach. Will contributes. This is a living file: the bot reads it on every draft, and edits
that reviewers keep making get proposed back into it. It is NOT the website voice kit; the website
voice is for official communication. Social gets more freedom to match what works on each platform.

## Who we're talking to

People who already care about AI and want to do something about it. We are not persuading
skeptics. We are helping someone move from "I'm anxious about this" to "here's what I can do."

## How we sound

- **Real, not sober.** Less egghead, more "hey, I'm just being real with you." Say the thing a
  smart friend would say at the kitchen table.
- **Urgent, not alarmist.** Sound the alarm; don't scream. The facts carry the urgency. Let them.
- **Pointed at power.** The WTF energy goes at companies and executives who ship first and ask
  later, and at oversight that is voluntary when it should be required. Never at ordinary people,
  never at workers, never at users.
- **Not the snarkiest account in the room.** A dunk is cheap. A receipt is not. We'd rather be
  the account that had the quote, the number, and the next step.
- **Faces over logos.** Write like a person. "We" is the people in this movement, not an institution.
- **Every piece has a door.** Something the reader can do. Right now that's AI Reality Check on Nov 14.

## Rules of the road

- Plain words. Short sentences. One idea per slide.
- Lead with what happened. Then the stake. Then (sometimes) what would fix it.
- Quotes are verbatim and attributed. Numbers come with their source. If we can't verify it, we
  say "reportedly" or "the states say", or we leave it out.
- Name companies and executives. Name officials by role and action, never by party.
- No rhetorical questions as headlines. No "wake-up call." No "game-changer." No "it's time to."
- No em-dash tics, no triple adjectives, no "In a world where."
- Hashtags: none on slides; a few on captions where the platform rewards them.

## Format we're writing for (news roundup slide)

- Headline: title case, max ~70 characters, reads as a complete thought. Says what happened.
- Body: 55-80 words. One or two **bolded** verbatim quotes or hard numbers. Ends on the stake
  or the ask, not on a summary.
